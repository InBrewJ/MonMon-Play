The SPOG Concept

What is MonMon?

MonMon is your financial cockpit built around the SPOG (Single Pane of Glass) methodology. It consolidates all checking accounts, credit lines, overdraft limits, recurring bills, and savings targets into one unified runway.

  • Left Per Day: Dynamic daily spending allowance calculated down to the penny based on your exact days remaining until payday.
  • Spending Pace & Baseline: Instant visual indicators showing if your current rate of spending is ahead of or behind your baseline pot.
  • Payday-to-Payday Cycle: Automatic bank holiday and working-day payday alignment.
  • Dynamic Savings Slider: Slide between 0% and 100% of income to live-slice funds between debt repayment and high-yield savings.

Grocery & Nutrition Intelligence

What is NomMon?

NomMon is MonMon's integrated meal and grocery planning assistant. It bridges the gap between your calculated weekly food pot and the supermarket aisle, transforming your budget into real, practical food you love.

  • Budget-Adaptive Intelligence: Automatically tunes recipes and grocery brands to match your exact budget tier—from frugal staples (Stamford Street Co., lentils, oats) to generous family dinners and luxury gourmet (Taste the Difference steaks, fresh salmon).
  • Sainsbury's Categorized Basket: Generates itemized shopping lists grouped by supermarket aisle with live price estimates.
  • Household Scaling: Scales seamlessly from solo budgets to feeding families of 2, 4, or more with accurate portion sizes.
  • Zero Guesswork: Eliminates food waste and prevents grocery overspending from blowing your monthly SPOG pot.
