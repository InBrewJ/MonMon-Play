Priority Queue & Interlocked Sliders Guide
  • Queue Priority (P1, P2...): Items are ranked in payoff order. P1 is the top-priority target. Use the ▲ and ▼ arrows in each row to shunt items up or down.
  • Interlocked Sliders: Adjusting any unlocked slider dynamically reallocates savings budget across other unlocked items so the total strictly matches your target pot.
  • Queue Rollover: When an item is paid off or deleted, 100% of its freed monthly budget automatically cascades into the highest-priority remaining item (P1).
